Common questions

Why are prayer times different from my local mosque?

Prayer times are calculated astronomically. Calculation methods, local conventions and adjustments can give different results. Check your city, time zone and calculation method in Evvâb Settings, and choose the method used by your local authority.

Why am I not receiving reminders?

Check that reminders and the individual prayers you want are enabled in Evvâb. In iPhone Settings, allow notifications for Evvâb and check Focus and notification-delivery settings. Reopen Evvâb after changing your location or reminder preferences. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

How do I keep my records when changing apps or phones?

Before deleting Evvâb or Expo Go, reinstalling, or moving to another phone, export a JSON backup from Evvâb Settings and save it somewhere you can access. Restore it from Settings in the new installation. Expo Go and a TestFlight or App Store installation use separate storage; your records do not transfer automatically.

How do I delete my information?

Use the data deletion control in Evvâb Settings. Deleting the app also removes its local app data. Separately delete any backup files you exported and manage any device backups in your Apple settings. See the privacy policy for details.

Is the qada estimate a religious ruling?

No. It is an editable planning aid based on information you enter, not a definitive calculation of prayers owed. For religious guidance, consult someone qualified whom you trust.
